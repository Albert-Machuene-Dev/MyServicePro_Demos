# Image & Content Permission Checklist

Before using a restaurant's Instagram, TikTok, or other content in a live client website, ensure you have proper permission.

## Copyright Basics
- Photos and videos are automatically protected by copyright when created.
- The restaurant may not own the copyright to content on their social media — the photographer or creator might.
- Giving credit is **not** a substitute for permission.

## Permission Checklist

- [ ] Ask the restaurant owner/manager for written permission to use their content.
- [ ] Ask them to confirm they own the copyright or have cleared rights with the photographer/creator.
- [ ] If people are identifiable in photos/videos, ensure consent was obtained (POPIA compliance in South Africa).
- [ ] Save the permission (email, WhatsApp screenshot, or signed note) in the project folder.
- [ ] If permission cannot be obtained, use AI-generated images or properly licensed stock photos instead.

## File to Keep Per Restaurant

Create `restaurants/<slug>/PERMISSION.md`:

```markdown
# Content Permission

**Restaurant:** [Name]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Permission obtained from:** [Name, Title]
**Method:** [Email / WhatsApp / In-person]
**Content allowed:** [Instagram photos / TikTok videos / Menu text / Logo]
**Notes:** [Any restrictions or credits required]
```

## Safe Default for Demos
For initial demos shown only on your tablet or an unlisted URL, risk is lower. But before making any site public or handing it to the client, complete this checklist.
