# MyServicePro In-Person Pitch Script

## Timing
- Best time: **2:30 PM – 4:30 PM**, after lunch rush and before dinner prep.
- Avoid lunch and dinner rushes completely.

## The 60-Second Script

**You:**
> "Hi, I'm Albert with MyServicePro. I know you are incredibly busy prepping for the dinner shift, so I will take exactly 60 seconds of your time."

*(Pause. Have your tablet already open and awake.)*

**You:**
> "I eat local, and I love what you guys do here, but I noticed your current website doesn't quite do your food justice. I actually went ahead and built a brand-new, modernized version of your site just to show you what's possible. Can I show you real quick?"

*(Turn the tablet toward them immediately. Let them see the demo.)*

**You:**
> "I focused on making the food look irresistible and made it super easy for people to find your menu and book a table on their phones. The buttons aren't active yet—this is just the vision."

*(Let them look. Watch their reaction.)*

**You:**
> "If you love this look, I can make it fully functional and live. You don't pay me a massive upfront fee to build it. I operate on a simple monthly subscription—I handle the hosting, the maintenance, the updates, and the heavy backend work. You just pay a flat monthly rate, and I take the whole digital headache off your plate. If it's not for you, absolutely no hard feelings, and I'll get out of your hair. What do you think of the design?"

## Why It Works
1. **Disarms immediately** — acknowledges they are busy.
2. **Show, don't tell** — shows a product that already belongs to their brand.
3. **Risk reversal** — no massive upfront fee, no hard feelings.

## Optional Backend Hooks (if they ask)
- Automated table reservations
- Direct-to-kitchen online ordering
- Customer database and analytics
- AI chatbot for bookings

## Closing Line
> "Which package feels right for where you are right now—the R999 Essential, the R1,799 Business Growth, or should we talk about the full Enterprise setup?"
